1. Welcome to GoHabit

By using GoHabit, you agree to these Terms of Service. GoHabit helps you organize personal habits, daily routines, and focus timers for self-improvement only — it does not provide medical, clinical, or professional advice.

2. Free Tier & GoHabit PRO Plans

You can use GoHabit for free within Free tier limits with Google AdMob banner ads. Upgrading to GoHabit PRO (1 Month, 6 Months, 12 Months, or Lifetime Access) unlocks unlimited habits, home-screen widgets, full history, PDF/CSV exports, streak freezes, and removes all ads.

3. Auto-Renewal & How to Cancel

Recurring plans (1 Month, 6 Months, and 12 Months) renew automatically through your Google Play account unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period. Lifetime Access is a single one-time payment and never auto-renews. You can manage or cancel anytime in Google Play Store -> Payments & subscriptions -> Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel an active subscription.

Manage Subscriptions Directly: You can manage or cancel your active GoHabit subscription anytime via the Google Play Subscriptions Center.

4. Free Trials & Introductory Offers

If your plan includes a free trial or discounted introductory price, your Google Play account will be charged automatically at the standard plan rate when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial expires. Trial eligibility is controlled by Google Play (typically one trial per Google Play account).

Strict Non-Refundable Policy

5. No Refunds Once Paid

All payments are final and non-refundable once charged. Once payment is completed for any plan (1 Month, 6 Months, 12 Months, or Lifetime Access), no refund will be initiated, and no partial or pro-rated refunds or credits are issued for unused time or mid-cycle cancellations, except where required by applicable law.

Important Subscription Details:
  • If you cancel a recurring plan, future renewals stop and your GoHabit PRO access will remain active until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • If a payment is reversed, disputed, or refunded by Google Play, GoHabit PRO benefits are removed immediately and your account returns to the Free tier.

6. Restoring Purchases on New Devices

Your GoHabit PRO purchase is linked to the Google Play account used at checkout. If you reinstall GoHabit or switch to another Android device, sign in with that same Google Play account and tap Restore Purchases in Profile or on the Paywall.

7. Your Habit Data & Backups

Your habits are stored locally on your device unless you turn on optional Google Drive appDataFolder backup. Before resetting your phone, clearing app storage, or uninstalling, save a cloud backup or local JSON/CSV export so you do not lose your data.
