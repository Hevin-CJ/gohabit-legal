Privacy Policy

Simple overview of how GoHabit keeps your habits on your phone and uses optional backup, ads, bug reports, and billing.

Effective Date: November 2026 • Application: GoHabit for Android (com.pavilion.gohabit)

1. Your Habits Stay on Your Phone

Your habits, streaks, routines, focus timers, and profile preferences are saved directly on your device using local Android Room storage. You do not need to create an online account to use GoHabit offline.

Privacy by Design: Your habit titles, check-in timestamps, notes, and progress metrics live entirely on your hardware and are never uploaded to any proprietary GoHabit server.

2. Optional Google Drive Backup

If you choose to connect your Google account for Cloud Backup, GoHabit uses only a private hidden app folder (appDataFolder) in your Google Drive to save and restore your habit backup file. GoHabit cannot see, edit, or read your personal photos, documents, or any other Google Drive files.

3. Ads on the Free Plan

To keep the Free plan free, GoHabit shows banner ads powered by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). AdMob may use your device Advertising ID and general device or region signals to show and measure ads. Upgrading to GoHabit PRO turns off all ads completely.

4. Crash & Bug Reports

GoHabit uses Firebase Crashlytics to send anonymous crash reports and basic device info (such as phone model and Android version) when an error occurs so bugs can be fixed faster. Crashlytics never includes your habit titles or personal notes.

5. Payments & Subscriptions

All GoHabit PRO purchases and subscriptions are handled securely by Google Play Billing and verified through RevenueCat so your PRO status and Restore Purchases work reliably. GoHabit never sees or stores your credit card, UPI, or bank account details.

6. App Security

GoHabit uses the Google Play Integrity check to confirm the app was downloaded genuinely from Google Play and to protect billing and PRO unlocks against tampering.

7. Your Choices & Control

You can export your habits (PDF, CSV, or JSON backup), delete local data by clearing device storage, or disconnect Google Drive anytime from your Google Account permissions. Active subscriptions can be managed or cancelled anytime in Google Play Store settings.
